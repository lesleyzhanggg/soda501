Riotous acts in various districts
*********************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of rioters continue to gather in Sha Tin, Tsuen Wan and Wong Tai Sin districts and commit destructive acts. In the past few hours, rioters had committed riotous acts in various districts in Hong Kong by setting fire, vandalising MTR stations, breaking glass panels of shops, throwing bricks and petrol bombs, and beating up MTR staff and bystanders, taking the law into their own hands.
Police appeal to all members of the public to stay in safe places and stay tuned to the latest situation.
Ends/Friday, October 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 23:51
