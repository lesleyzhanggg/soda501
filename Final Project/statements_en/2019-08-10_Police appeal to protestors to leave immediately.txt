Police appeal to protestors to leave immediately
Police appeal to protestors to leave immediately
************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Since 9pm yesterday (August 9), around 200 protestors have been gathering outside the Wong Tai Sin Disciplined Services Quarters at the junction of Shatin Pass Road and Muk Lun Street, behaving in a disorderly manner and shining laser beams at the police officers. Police officers are on guard at scene.
Police appeal to the protesters to stop their provocative acts. Residents in the vicinity should leave immediately and mind their own safety.
Ends/Saturday, August 10, 2019
Issued at HKT 0:11
