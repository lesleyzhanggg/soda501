Protestors blocked roads in Tsim Sha Tsui and Report Room service suspended
***************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 9pm, some protestors blocked carriageways in Nathan Road near Park Lane Shopper's Boulevard in Tsim Sha Tsui, paralysing the traffic. The Report Room services of Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station are now temporarily suspended. Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave the scene immediately.
Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Saturday, August 31, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:23
