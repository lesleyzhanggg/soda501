Cancellation of LegCo Bills Committee meetings
**********************************************
The following is issued on behalf of the Legislative Council Secretariat:
The Legislative Council Bills Committee on Freight Containers (Safety) (Amendment) Bill 2019 and Bills Committee on Fisheries Protection (Amendment) Bill 2019 have not been formed. The first meetings of the Bills Committees originally scheduled for 8.30am tomorrow (May 20) have been cancelled.
Ends/Tuesday, May 19, 2020
Issued at HKT 14:30
