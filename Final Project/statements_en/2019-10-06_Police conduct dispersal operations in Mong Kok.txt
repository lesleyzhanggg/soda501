Police conduct dispersal operations in Mong Kok
***********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Protestors have been participating in unlawful assemblies and blocking major roads in Mong Kok area. Police is now conducting a dispersal operation in Mong Kok. Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately. Members of the public are advised to stay at home and not to go out unless it is necessary.
Ends/Sunday, October 6, 2019
Issued at HKT 17:37
