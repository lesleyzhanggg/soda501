Service of Commandant of Hong Kong Auxiliary Police Force extended
******************************************************************
The Government today (December 27) announced the approval of the extension of the service of Mr Yang Joe-tsi, as the Commandant of the Hong Kong Auxiliary Police Force (HKAPF) for two years up to January 6, 2022.
Mr Yang first joined the HKAPF in April 1989 and was appointed as the Commandant in April 2016.
Mr Yang has led the HKAPF with excellence. He is deeply committed to the HKAPF and has been providing stable leadership for the HKAPF to cope with the increasingly diverse responsibilities.
