LD launches exhibition on Employment Ordinance and Minimum Wage Ordinance
*************************************************************************
Members of the public are invited to an exhibition on the Employment Ordinance and the Minimum Wage Ordinance organised by the Labour Department in Southern District on January 11 and 12 (Saturday and Sunday).
The exhibition will feature the main provisions of the Employment Ordinance and the Minimum Wage Ordinance, as well as good human resource management measures, employment rights and benefits for foreign domestic helpers. Related publications and souvenirs will be distributed and promotional videos will be shown.
The exhibition will be held at 1/F, West Commercial Block, Marina Square, South Horizons, Ap Lei Chau, Aberdeen, Hong Kong from 11am to 6pm. Admission is free.
Ends/Thursday, January 9, 2020
