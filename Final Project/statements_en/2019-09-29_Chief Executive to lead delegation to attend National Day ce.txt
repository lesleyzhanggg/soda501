Chief Executive to lead delegation to attend National Day celebration activities in Beijing
Chief Executive to lead delegation to attend National Day celebration activities in Beijing
******************************************************************************************
The Chief Executive, Mrs Carrie Lam, will lead a delegation comprising more than 240 members from various sectors of the community to Beijing tomorrow (September 30) to participate in activities to celebrate the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China.
Mrs Lam will return to Hong Kong in the evening of October 1 via Shenzhen. During her absence, the Chief Secretary for Administration, Mr Matthew Cheung Kin-chung, will be the Acting Chief Executive.
Ends/Sunday, September 29, 2019
