Police appeal to public not to go to Wan Chai and Admiralty
***********************************************************
At about 7.30am this morning (July 1), a large number of protestors charged Police cordon lines in Wan Chai and Admiralty, hence, Police conducted disperse actions.
Police appeal protestors not to charge Police cordon lines and leave as soon as possible. Due to the chaotic situations in Wan Chai and Admiralty, Police appeal the public not to go to these areas.
Ends/Monday, July 1, 2019
