Wan Chai Police Station Report Room service suspended
*****************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
The Report Room service of Wan Chai Police Station is now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicles access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, August 11, 2019
Issued at HKT 18:10
