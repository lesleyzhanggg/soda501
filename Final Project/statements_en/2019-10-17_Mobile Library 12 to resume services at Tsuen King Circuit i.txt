Mobile Library 12 to resume services at Tsuen King Circuit in Tsuen Wan
***********************************************************************
The services provided by Mobile Library 12 at Tsuen King Circuit in Tsuen Wan will resume on Saturday (October 19) after completion of refurbishment work at the venue, a spokesman for the Leisure and Cultural Services Department announced today (October 17).
For enquiries about the services of Mobile Library 12, please call 2479 1055.
Ends/Thursday, October 17, 2019
