Protestors in Causeway Bay participating in an unauthorized assembly
********************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 2pm today (September 15), protestors gathering at Causeway Bay are blocking the carriageways at Hennessy Road and are committing the offence of “Participating in an unauthorized assembly”.
Police appeal to members of the public to leave immediately. Due to the obstruction of traffic, drivers are advised to stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Sunday, September 15, 2019
Issued at HKT 14:43
