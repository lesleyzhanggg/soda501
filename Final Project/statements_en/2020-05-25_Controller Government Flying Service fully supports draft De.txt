Controller, Government Flying Service fully supports draft Decision on establishing and improving legal system and enforcement mechanisms for HKSAR to safeguard national security
******************************************************************************************
The National People's Congress (NPC) will deliberate a draft Decision on establishing and improving the legal system and enforcement mechanisms for the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) to safeguard national security (the Decision).
The Controller of the Government Flying Service, Captain Wu Wai-hung, supports the Decision. The Government Flying Service (GFS), as an aviation force of the HKSAR Government, particularly provides aviation support for various disciplinary forces to carry out law enforcement work in a swift and effective manner. The GFS will hereto fully support the HKSAR Government to perform its due responsibilities to safeguard national security.
During the past year, it has been witnessed that the violence in Hong Kong has been escalating and the threat and damage to the lives and property of Hong Kong citizens have been increasing. Terrorism is also growing in Hong Kong and activities jeopardising national security are becoming rampant, which fill society with violence and unrest.
​The GFS is ready to support the Police to take resolute enforcement actions and will fully support the HKSAR Government to implement the Decision of the NPC with a view to establishing a safe and stable society and ensuring the long-term prosperity, stability and peace of Hong Kong.
Ends/Monday, May 25, 2020
