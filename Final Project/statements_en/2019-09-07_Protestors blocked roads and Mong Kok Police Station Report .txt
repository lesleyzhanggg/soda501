Protestors blocked roads and Mong Kok Police Station Report Room service suspended
Protestors blocked roads and Mong Kok Police Station Report Room service suspended
**********************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some protestors are currently surrounding Mong Kok Police Station, and aiming laser beams at police officers.  They are setting up barricades, occupying the carriageways and causing serious obstruction to traffic.  The report room services of Mong Kok Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and to leave immediately.
Ends/Saturday, September 7, 2019
Issued at HKT 20:54
