Rioters hurl objects into rail track and block road
R
ioters hurl objects into rail track and block road
***************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 3pm, rioters hurled objects into the rail track of Sha Tin MTR station. Such acts neglect the safety of members of the public as the station was in operation at the material time.
More, radical protestors blocked Nathan Road near Argyle Street in Mong Kong with barricades, paralysing the traffic in the vicinity.
Police warn the rioters to stop their illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Sunday, October 13, 2019
Issued at HKT 15:45
