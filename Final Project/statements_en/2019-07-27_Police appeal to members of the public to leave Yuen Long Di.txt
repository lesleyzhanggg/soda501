Police appeal to members of the public to leave Yuen Long District as soon as possible
Police appeal to members of the public to leave Yuen Long District as soon as possible
**************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Police are aware that in Yuen Long area, some protestors were gathering in Nam Pin Wai, behaving in a disorderly manner. Given that some protestors were holding iron poles, self-made shields and even removing fences from roads, Police are concerned about possible deterioration of the situation. Police appeal to members of the public to stay calm and leave the area as soon as possible as a chaotic scene may ensue within a short period of time.
Ends/Saturday, July 27, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:57
