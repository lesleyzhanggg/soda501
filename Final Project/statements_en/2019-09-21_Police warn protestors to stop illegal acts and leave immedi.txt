Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts and leave immediately
*****************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Radical protestors today (September 21) are holding offensive weapons including metal rods, slingshots and laser guns near Tuen Mun Government Offices. The protestors set fire on a national flag, damaged properties and set barricades. Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Saturday, September 21, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:28
