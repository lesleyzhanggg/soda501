New mobile library service point to be set up at Sheung Shui Wai in Sheung Shui
*******************************************************************************
A new service point for Mobile Library 12 of the Leisure and Cultural Services Department will be set up at Tung Hing Road Car Park, Sheung Shui Wai, Sheung Shui from November 4 (Monday).
The opening hours of the new service point will be from 2.45pm to 6pm every alternate Monday (except library closing days and public holidays).
For enquiries about Mobile Library 12 services, please call 2479 1055.
Ends/Tuesday, October 29, 2019
