Government clarifies rumours about construction of police base for counter-terrorism
************************************************************************************
In response to rumours on social media on the Internet that the Government plans to construct a police base near San Uk Ling for counter-terrorism, a Government spokesman today (November 27) clarified again that the Government does not have such a plan. The claims are totally unfounded.
Ends/Wednesday, November 27, 2019
