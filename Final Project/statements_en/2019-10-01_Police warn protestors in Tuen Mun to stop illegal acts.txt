Police warn protestors in Tuen Mun to stop illegal acts
*******************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of violent protestors outside Tuen Mun Town Hall have set up barricades, damaged properties and attacked Police at scene. Police will use appropriate force to disperse the protestors. Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Tuesday, October 1, 2019
Issued at HKT 14:41
