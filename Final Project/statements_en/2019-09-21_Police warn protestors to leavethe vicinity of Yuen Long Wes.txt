Police warn protestors to leave the vicinity of Yuen Long West Rail Station immediately
Police warn protestors to leave
the vicinity of Yuen Long West Rail Station
immediately
***************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large number of protestors gathered in the vicinity of Yuen Long West Rail Station. They blocked the roads and set fire. Some even threw petrol bombs to Police vehicle, causing danger to the life of the Police officers onboard. Police will shortly disperse the protestors. Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave the scene immediately. Members of the public are advised to avoid travelling to Yuen Long West Rail Station area.
Ends/Saturday, September 21, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:52
