Protestors occupy Nathan Road again
***********************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 4.30pm today (May 27), protestors occupied Nathan Road in Mong Kok again. They blocked the carriageways with trash, paper boxes and traffic signs. Police officers sped to the scene and took enforcement action. Some protestors set up barricades with umbrellas. Police are now removing obstacles on the carriageways and will reopen them as soon as possible.
Protestors occupied and blocked Nathan Road at around 2pm. Officers took action and successfully restored public order. Police will continue to take resolute enforcement action to arrest lawbreakers.
Ends/Wednesday, May 27, 2020
Issued at HKT 19:54
