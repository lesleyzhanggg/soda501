Secretary for Justice to sign framework arrangement on legal exchange and mutual learning in Shenzhen
******************************************************************************************
The Secretary for Justice, Ms Teresa Cheng, SC, will tomorrow (September 7) depart for Shenzhen to sign a framework arrangement with leaders of the High People's Court of Guangdong Province on exchange and mutual learning in legal aspects between Hong Kong and Guangdong (the Arrangement). The Arrangement aims to strengthen legal exchange and collaboration in the Greater Bay Area. She will then attend a seminar organised by the Shenzhen Intermediate People's Court and the Department of Justice under the Arrangement.
Ms Cheng will return to Hong Kong on the same day.
