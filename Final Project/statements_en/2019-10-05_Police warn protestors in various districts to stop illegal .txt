Police warn protestors in various districts to stop illegal acts and report room services of Yuen Long Police Station suspended
Police warn protestors in various districts to stop illegal acts and report room services of Yuen Long Police Station suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Protestors have staged hit-and-run demonstrations in districts like Aberdeen, Siu Sai Wan, Yau Ma Tei, Mong Kok, Tseung Kwan O, Tai Po, Yuen Long and Tin Shui Wai. They blocked roads extensively, vandalised shops and public properties, seriously disturbing the public peace. Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately. Members of the public in these areas are advised to leave as soon as possible and mind their own safety.
The report room services of Yuen Long Police Station are now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicle access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Saturday, October 5, 2019
Issued at HKT 22:31
