Protestors block Nathan Road and Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station Report Room services suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Since around 3.30pm, a large group of protestors have been gathering on Nathan Road of Tsim Sha Tsui and heading towards Mong Kok. They have also set barricades along the route. This has caused serious obstruction and jeopardized emergency services for the public.
The protestors are participating in an unauthorized assembly, which constitutes an offence under the Hong Kong Laws. The Police appeal to the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately.
Due to safety reasons, the Report Room services of the Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station are temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Saturday, October 12, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:38
