Police conduct dispersal operation in North Point
Police conduct dispersal operation in North Point
*************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Since 9 pm, some radical protestors have been gathering and occupying King’s Road, North Point, obstructing traffic. Having given repeated warnings but in vain, Police officers are now conducting a dispersal operation with the deployment of tear gas. Police appeal to the protestors concerned and other citizens to leave as soon as possible.
Police remind residents in the vicinity to keep their windows closed, if necessary, and stay indoors.
Ends/Sunday, September 15, 2019
Issued at HKT 23:18
