Senior appointment in Police Force
**********************************
Former Deputy Commissioner of Police (Operations) Lau Yip-shing has been appointed to a temporary post of Deputy Commissioner of Police (Special Duties) commencing today (August 9).  The main duties of the supernumerary post are to assist the Commissioner of Police in handling large-scale public order events, and steering the forthcoming major operations, including the celebratory activities in relation to the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People’s Republic of China.
Ends/Friday, August 9, 2019
