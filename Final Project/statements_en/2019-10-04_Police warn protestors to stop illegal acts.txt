Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts
Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts
********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Violent protestors today (October 4) have committed destructive acts extensively across Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and the New Territories, such as setting fires and breaking glass panels of shops. They also vandalised facilities at Sha Tin and Kwun Tong MTR Stations.
A large group of radical protestors have been blocking Lung Cheung Road, Wong Tai Sin, paralysing traffic in the vicinity. The report room services of Wong Tai Sin Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please dial 999.
Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Friday, October 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:20
