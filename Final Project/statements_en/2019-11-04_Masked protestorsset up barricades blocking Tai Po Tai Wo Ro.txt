***************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At night, some masked protestors flash mobbed Tai Po Tai Wo Road. They built barricades with miscellaneous objects including public light bus stands, rubbish bins and railings, blocking the carriageways. The masked protestors then fled. Police arrived at scene to clear the barricades and conduct patrol in the vicinity. Police urge the protestors to stop all illegal acts.
Ends/Monday, November 4, 2019
