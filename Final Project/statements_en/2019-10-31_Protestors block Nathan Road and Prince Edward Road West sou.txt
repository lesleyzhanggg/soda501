Protestors block Nathan Road and Prince Edward Road West southbound carriageway
*******************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At 7pm today (October 31), protestors blocked Nathan Road and Prince Edward Road West southbound carriageway in Mong Kok, paralysing traffic. Having given repeated warnings to the protestors in vain, Police officers have deployed tear gas and will use minimum necessary force to disperse protestors.
Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts. Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Thursday, October 31, 2019
Issued at HKT 19:39
