Police to conduct dispersal operation on Hong Kong Island
Police to conduct dispersal operation on Hong Kong Island
*********************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Protestors have been participating in unlawful assemblies, blocking major roads at multiple locations. Police will soon conduct dispersal operations on Hong Kong Island and warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately.  Members of the public are advised to stay indoors and keep their windows shut.
Ends/Sunday, October 6, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:59
