Government strongly condemns violent and vandalistic acts
Government strongly condemns violent and vandalistic acts
*********************************************************
The radical protesters today (September 28) blocked roads, vandalised public property and set fires in the vicinity of the Central Government Offices (CGO) as well as attacked the CGO and police officers at the scene with bricks and petrol bombs, disrupting the traffic and damaging the building, jeopardising the safety of the police officers at the scene and seriously breaching the public peace. A Government spokesman strongly condemns the violent and vandalistic acts of the radical protesters.
Ends/Saturday, September 28, 2019
