47 more nomination forms for District Council Ordinary Election received
************************************************************************
A total of 47 nomination forms for the 2019 District Council Ordinary Election were received by the Returning Officers of the 18 districts today (October 15).
This brings the total number of nomination forms received to 1006 since the start of the nomination period on October 4, which will continue until October 17.
The Returning Officer of the Sham Shui Po District today received a notice of withdrawal of candidature from a nominee for the poll in Po Lai Constituency.
