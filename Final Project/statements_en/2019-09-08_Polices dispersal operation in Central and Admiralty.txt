Police's dispersal operation in Central and Admiralty
Police's dispersal operation in Central and Admiralty
*****************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors are currently gathering in Central and Admiralty area.  They set fire in multiple locations in Central, damaged facilities of Central MTR Station, and causing obstruction to the traffic.
After repeated but futile warnings, in face of the situation, Police officers are using appropriate force to disperse protestors.
Police appeal to everyone in the area to leave immediately, and to avoid travelling to the area.
Ends/Sunday, September 8, 2019
Issued at HKT 18:26
