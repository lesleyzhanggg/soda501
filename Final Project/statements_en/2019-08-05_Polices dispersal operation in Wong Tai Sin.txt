Police's dispersal operation in Wong Tai Sin
Police's dispersal operation in Wong Tai Sin
********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors are currently surrounding Wong Tai Sin Police Station and Wong Tai Sin Disciplined Services Quarters, hurling suspected corrosive substances at Police officers, posing a serious threat to the safety of everyone at scene.
In face of the situation, Police will soon deploy tear gas to disperse protestors.
Police appeal to everyone at scene to leave immediately and strongly condemn the violent acts of protestors.
Ends/Monday, August 5, 2019
Issued at HKT 1:01
