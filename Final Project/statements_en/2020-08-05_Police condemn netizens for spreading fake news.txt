Police condemn netizens for spreading fake news
Police condemn netizens for spreading fake news
***********************************************
Police are aware that this afternoon (August 5), some netizens spread the fake news that the Commissioner of Police was infected with COVID-19. The Force sternly state that such a rumour is totally groundless and fictitious, and severely condemn the ill-intentioned parties who intend to cause chaos by spreading fake news. Resolute follow-up action will be taken.
Ends/Wednesday, August 5, 2020
