Traffic enforcement operation against illegal parking in Sha Tin Police District
********************************************************************************
Sha Tin Police District launched a traffic enforcement operation against illegal parking in Sha Tin, Tin Sum and Ma On Shan police divisions today (September 21). The operation will continue on September 22, 27 and 28.
To facilitate face-to-face class resumption of schools, stringent enforcement actions against illegal parking or stopping will be taken at traffic black spots near schools.
The Police will issue multiple tickets and take summons actions against drivers who have contravened illegal parking regulations, obstructed traffic flow or caused danger to other road users. Illegally parked vehicles may also be towed away immediately.
