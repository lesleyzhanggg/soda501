Protestors block roads in Tsim Sha Tsui and Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station Report Room services suspended
Protestors block roads in Tsim Sha Tsui and Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station Report Room services suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors have been occupying carriageways near Salisbury Road, Canton Road, Chatham Road in Tsim Sha Tsui.  At Austin Road West and Jordan Road, some radical protestors are setting barricades, blocking roads, and seriously obstructing traffic.
They are participating in an unauthorized assembly.  Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts immediately.
The Report Room services of the Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station are temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, October 20, 2019
Issued at HKT 14:37
