Relocation of Ngau Tau Kok Police Station and Traffic Kowloon East report rooms (with photos)
******************************************************************************************
The Ngau Tau Kok Police Station and the Traffic Kowloon East report rooms will be relocated from 1 Siu Yip Street, Kowloon Bay to 105 Concorde Road, Kai Tak on September 23 and October 6, 2020 respectively.
The new Ngau Tau Kok Police Station Report Room will commence service from 7am on September 23, and simultaneously all public services at the old Ngau Tau Kok Police Station Report Room will cease.
The new Traffic Kowloon East Report Room will commence service from 1pm on October 6, and simultaneously the old report room will cease operation.
For enquiries, please call Ngau Tau Kok Police Station Report Room at 3661 1626 or Traffic Kowloon East Report Room at 3661 1636.
Ends/Friday, September 18, 2020
