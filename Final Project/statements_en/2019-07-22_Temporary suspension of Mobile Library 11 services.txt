Temporary suspension of Mobile Library 11 services
**************************************************
Mobile Library 11 will suspend services from today (July 22) to July 24 for urgent repair works, a spokesman for the Leisure and Cultural Services Department announced today.
The affected service points are Lai Yiu Estate in Kwai Chung, Wan Tau Tong Estate and Tai Yuen Estate in Tai Po, and Kwong Yuen Estate in Sha Tin. For enquiries about Mobile Library 11 services, please call 2479 1055.
Readers are welcome to use other public libraries during the service suspension period. They may also renew library materials by telephoning 2698 0002 or 2827 2833, or via
www.hkpl.gov.hk
.
