Protestors occupy carriageways near Hennessy Road and Percival Street
**********************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Protestors continue to occupy the area in the vicinity of Percival Street and Hennessy Road, paralysing traffic. At 8.30pm, protestors started to face off with the Police and on multiple occasions, had scuffles with people who held different views. Police officers are using appropriate force to disperse the protestors.
Police warn all protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave the site immediately. Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stayed tuned to the latest traffic situation.
Ends/Sunday, September 29, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:28
