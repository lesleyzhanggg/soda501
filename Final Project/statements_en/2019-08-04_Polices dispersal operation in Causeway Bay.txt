Police's dispersal operation in Causeway Bay
Police's dispersal operation in Causeway Bay
********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
The violent protestors gathering in Causeway Bay area continued to block roads extensively, maliciously damaging property and disregarding law and order.
After repeated but futile warnings, in face of the situation, Police deployed tear gas in the junction of Hennessy Road and Percival Street, using minimum force to disperse the protestors.
Police appeal to residents of the area to stay tuned to the latest situation and if necessary, keep their windows shut and stay indoors.
Ends/Sunday, August 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:52
