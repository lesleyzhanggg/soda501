Policy Address" available to public on Wednesday
************************************************************************
The Chief Executive, Mrs Carrie Lam, will release "The Chief Executive's 2019 Policy Address" on Wednesday (October 16).
The full text of the Policy Address will be released at
www.policyaddress.gov.hk
after the Chief Executive has completed her speech.
Copies of the Policy Address and other related publications will be available for public collection from the time the Chief Executive has completed her speech to 7pm at the 20 Home Affairs Enquiry Centres (HAECs) of the Home Affairs Department, except for the Mui Wo and Cheung Chau HAECs, which will close at 6pm and 6.45pm respectively.
