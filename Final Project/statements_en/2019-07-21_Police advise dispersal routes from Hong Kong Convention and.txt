Police advise dispersal routes from Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (1)
Police advise dispersal routes from Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (1)
**********************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Police advise the public to consider the following dispersal routes from Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (HKCEC):
1) take public transport at the bus terminus of Convention Avenue from the east of HKCEC;
2) take ferry or other public transport at Wan Chai Ferry Pier*;
3) walk towards the direction of Causeway Bay.
* The ferry service between Wan Chai and Tsim Sha Tsui has been strengthened since 2pm. Members of the public can choose to leave Wan Chai by ferry service.
Ends/Sunday, July 21, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:59
