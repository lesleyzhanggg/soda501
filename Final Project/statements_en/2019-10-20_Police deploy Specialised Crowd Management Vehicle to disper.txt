Police deploy Specialised Crowd Management Vehicle to disperse rioters along Nathan Road
****************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
In response to the rioter’s illegal acts, Police are now using the minimum force necessary, including the Specialised Crowd Management Vehicle, to disperse rioters along Nathan Road.
Police warn the rioters to stop their illegal acts immediately.
Ends/Sunday, October 20, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:31
