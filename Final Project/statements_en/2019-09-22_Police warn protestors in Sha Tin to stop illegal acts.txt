Police warn protestors in Sha Tin to stop illegal acts
******************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some radical protestors are recklessly damaging the facilities with hard objects, spraying water on the floor and throwing objects at Sha Tin MTR Station.
In view of the current situation, the report room services of Sha Tin Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Police warn the protestors to stop illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Sunday, September 22, 2019
Issued at HKT 17:20
