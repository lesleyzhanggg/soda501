Masked protestors gather
at different districts
***********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Since 2pm today (October 13), various groups of masked protestors have been gathering at malls at different districts including Tai Po, Tsing Yi, Sha Tin, Kwun Tong, Kowloon Bay, Tsuen Wan, Tuen Mun and Tai Koo. Some of them have sprayed paint and caused damages to the facilities therein.
Members of the public are reminded to mind their personal safety and leave the scene immediately in case of danger. Police hereby reiterate that the protestors participating in the unauthorised assembly are committing an offence under the Hong Kong Laws.
Ends/Sunday, October 13, 2019
Issued at HKT 15:24
