Protestors occupying carriageways in Causeway Bay
P
rotestors occupying carriageways in Causeway Bay
*************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors are blocking the carriageways in Hennessy Road near Yee Wo Street, seriously paralysing the traffic. The protestors are participating in an unauthorized assembly. Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and respect other people’s rights while expressing their views.
Police appeal to drivers to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangements.
The report room services of Wan Chai Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, October 6, 2019
Issued at HKT 14:12
