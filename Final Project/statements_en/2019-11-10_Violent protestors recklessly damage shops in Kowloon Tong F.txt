Violent protestors recklessly damage shops in Kowloon Tong Festival Walk
************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 4.30pm today (November 10), some violent protestors recklessly damaged shops in Kowloon Tong Festival Walk. To stop the crime, some Police officers attended scene and arrested several offenders. During the arrest, a large group of protestors were clamouring, some even attacked police officers, resulted in the escape of the arrested person. Police officers therefore used minimum necessary force to effect the arrest action.
Police express condemnation against the violent protestors and warn them to leave immediately and stop all violent acts. As some violent protestors are still gathering in the mall, Police appeal to bystanders to leave immediately. Members of the public are advised to mind their own safety and stay tuned to announcements of the mall.
Ends/Sunday, November 10, 2019
Issued at HKT 18:10
