Latest arrangements for exhibition on Employment Ordinance and Minimum Wage Ordinance
Latest arrangements
for
exhibition on Employment Ordinance and Minimum Wage Ordinance
*************************************************************************************
The Labour Department announced that in consideration of circumstances nearby and traffic situations, the exhibition on Employment Ordinance and Minimum Wage Ordinance held today (October 5) at the sales venue 2, G/F, Homantin Plaza, 80 Fat Kwong Street, Ho Man Tin will be closed at 3pm.
The exhibition originally scheduled for tomorrow (October 6) will be cancelled.
Ends/Saturday, October 5, 2019
