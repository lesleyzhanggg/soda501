Town Planning Board Secretariat's Reception Counter maintains services for collection of submissions made under the Town Planning Ordinance
******************************************************************************************
The following is issued on behalf of the Town Planning Board:
In light of the extension of special work arrangement for government departments and the latest development of the novel coronavirus infection , members of the public can place their submissions made under the Town Planning Ordinance, into the drop-in box located at the Town Planning Board Secretariat's reception counter at 15/F, North Point Government Offices during office hours (9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday) starting from next Monday (February 3).  Other counter services will continue to be temporarily suspended until further notice.
For enquiries, please call the Secretariat on 2231 4810.
Ends/Saturday, February 1, 2020
