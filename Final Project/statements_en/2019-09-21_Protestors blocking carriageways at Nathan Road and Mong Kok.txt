Protestors blocking carriageways at Nathan Road and Mong Kok Police Station Report Room service suspended
Protestors blocking carriageways at Nathan Road and Mong Kok Police Station Report Room service suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some protestors were blocking carriageways at Nathan Road in Mong Kok. They set up barricades with trash bins and debris, paralysing the traffic. Police dispersal operations are still in progress.
The report room services of Mong Kok Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please dial 999.
Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave the scene immediately. Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Saturday, September 21, 2019
Issued at HKT 22:49
