Violent protestors gather at Tai Hing Operational Base in Tuen Mun and block roads
**********************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Starting from 8.30pm tonight (October 28), a group of violent protestors have gathered outside the Tai Hing Operational Base, Tsun Wen Road, Tuen Mun, shouting and aiming laser beams at police officers. The protestors have also blocked Leung Wan Street with removed railings and traffic cones, affecting the traffic at scene.
Police condemn all violent acts and appeal to everyone at scene to stop all illegal acts immediately.
Ends/Monday, October 28, 2019
Issued at HKT 22:26
