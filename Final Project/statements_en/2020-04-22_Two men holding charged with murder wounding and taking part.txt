Two men holding charged with murder, wounding and taking part in a riot
***********************************************************************
Police today (April 22) laid a holding charge against two men, aged 16 and 17 respectively, jointly with one count of murder, one count of wounding and one count of taking part in a riot.
The two men were in suspected connection with a murder case happened on November 13, 2019 in Sheung Shui in which a man was killed.
The case will be mentioned at Tuen Mun Magistrates’ Courts today.
Ends/Wednesday, April 22, 2020
