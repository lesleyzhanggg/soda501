Police take resolute enforcement actions against rioters setting barricades in Wong Tai Sin
Police take resolute enforcement actions against rioters setting barricades in Wong Tai Sin
******************************************************************************************
Around 8pm last night (February 12), rioters occupied Po Kong Village Road, Wong Tai Sin and set barricades, seriously paralysing traffic and obstructing emergency services. Some of them even threw rubbish at roads, endangering public health and undermining the efforts of fighting epidemic.
In face of their illegal acts, Police attended scene and took resolute enforcement actions to restore the traffic and public order promptly. Police condemn all illegal acts.
Ends/Thursday, February 13, 2020
