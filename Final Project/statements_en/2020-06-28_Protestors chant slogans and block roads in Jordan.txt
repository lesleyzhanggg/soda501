Protestors chant slogans and block roads in Jordan
**************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At about 3pm today (June 28), protestors travelled from Jordan towards Yau Ma Tei. Police warned the protestors in the vicinity of Kansu Street, Yau Ma Tei that participating in such events may contravene "taking part in an unauthorised assembly" in accordance with the Public Order Ordinance.
Police warn all protestors to stop occupying the carriageways as such an act endangers the safety of the protestors themselves and the safety of other road users. Police will take resolute enforcement actions and arrest operation if anyone refuses to cooperate or defies the spirit of the law.
Ends/Sunday, June 28, 2020
Issued at HKT 16:48
