Police warn protestors in Sha Tin to stop illegal acts
Police warn protestors in Sha Tin to stop illegal acts
******************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large groups of radical protestors have been blocking Sha Tin Rural Committee Road & Yuen Wo Road. They have also built barricades and paralysed traffic in the vicinity.
Having given repeated warnings in vain, Police officers have deployed tear gas and appropriate force to disperse the protestors.
Police appeal to everyone at scene to leave immediately. Residents of the area are advised to stay tuned to the latest situation and if necessary, keep their windows shut and stay indoors.
Ends/Tuesday, October 1, 2019
Issued at HKT 15:11
