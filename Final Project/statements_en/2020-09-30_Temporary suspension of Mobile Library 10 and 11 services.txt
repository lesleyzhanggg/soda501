Temporary suspension of Mobile Library 10 and 11 services
*********************************************************
Mobile Library 10 will suspend services from today, while the service point of Mobile Library 11 at Cheung Ching Estate in Tsing Yi will be suspended from October 15 until further notice, a spokesman for the Leisure and Cultural Services Department announced today (September 30).
Mobile Library 10 will suspend services from today until further notice for urgent repair works. The affected service points are Lung Mun Oasis, Gold Coast and Tsing Tin Playground in Tuen Mun; Kwai Chung Estate and Cheung Wang Estate in Kwai Tsing; Tin Shui Estate and Tin Yuet Estate in Tin Shui Wai; Fung Cheung Road and Hung Shui Kiu in Yuen Long; Ching Ho Estate in Sheung Shui; and Hoi Lai Estate in Sham Shui Po. For enquiries about Mobile Library 10 services, please call 2450 1857.
The services provided by Mobile Library 11 at Cheung Ching Estate in Tsing Yi will be suspended from October 15 until further notice to facilitate maintenance work for the external wall at the venue. For enquiries about Mobile Library 11 services, please call 2479 1055.
Readers are welcome to use other public libraries during the service suspension periods. They may also renew library materials by telephoning 2698 0002 or 2827 2833, or via
www.hkpl.gov.hk
.
