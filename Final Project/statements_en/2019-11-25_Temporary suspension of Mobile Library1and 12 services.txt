Temporary suspension of Mobile Library 1 and 12 services
Temporary suspension of Mobile Library
1
and 12 services
********************************************************
Mobile Libraries 1 and 12 will suspend services during designated periods in December and January next year for routine maintenance, a spokesman for the Leisure and Cultural Services Department announced today (November 25).
Mobile Library 1 will suspend services from December 2 to 14. The affected service points are Whampoa Garden in Hung Hom, Riviera Gardens in Tsuen Wan, Shek Lei (I) Estate in Kwai Chung, Ko Yee Estate in Yau Tong, Kai Ching Estate in Kowloon City, Tsui Chuk Garden in Wong Tai Sin and Park Island in Ma Wan. For enquiries about Mobile Library 1 services, please call 2414 3157.
Mobile Library 12 will suspend services from December 30 to January 11 next year. The affected service points are Kam Tsin Village and Sheung Shui Wai Tung Hing Road Car Park in Sheung Shui; Ka Fuk Estate and Cheong Shing Court in Fanling; Lei Muk Shue Estate, Bayview Garden, Sham Tseng Market Carpark, Tsuen King Circuit Playground and Cheung Shan Estate in Tsuen Wan; Tai Wo Hau Estate and Kwai Luen Estate in Kwai Chung; Kwong Fuk Estate in Tai Po; and Fu Cheong Estate in Sham Shui Po. For enquiries about Mobile Library 12 services, please call 2479 1055.
Readers are welcome to use other public libraries during the service suspension periods. They may also renew library materials by telephoning 2698 0002 or 2827 2833, or via
www.hkpl.gov.hk
.
