Tenders invited for Site Formation and Infrastructure Works for Police Facilities in Kong Nga Po
******************************************************************************************
​The Civil Engineering and Development Department (CEDD) today (June 21) invited tenders for the contract for Site Formation and Infrastructure Works for Police Facilities in Kong Nga Po (Contract No. ND/2018/01).
The works are scheduled to commence in November 2019 and will take about 38 months to complete. The works mainly comprise:
* site formation of about 19 hectares of land at Kong Nga Po;
* upgrading of the existing Kong Nga Po Road of about 1.7 kilometres long to a 7.3-metre-wide single two-lane carriageway and construction of a new vehicular bridge of about 100m long; and
* associated infrastructure works, including road works, waterworks, drainage and sewerage works, geotechnical works, landscaping works, environmental mitigation measures and other ancillary works.
The CEDD has appointed AECOM Asia Company Limited as the consultant to design and supervise the works. Interested contractors may obtain the forms of tender and further particulars from the company at 8/F, Grand Central Plaza, Tower 2, 138 Sha Tin Rural Committee Road, Sha Tin.
The invitation to tender was gazetted today. The closing time for the tender is noon on August 16, 2019.
Details of the tender notice are available at the CEDD's website (
www.cedd.gov.hk/eng/tender/contract/tender/index.html
). For enquiries, please call AECOM Asia Company Limited at 3922 9000 during office hours.
Ends/Friday, June 21, 2019
