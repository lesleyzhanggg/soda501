Protesters occupying Hennessy Road in Causeway Bay
**************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large number of protestors are occupying Hennessy Road in Causeway Bay near SOGO, seriously paralysing the traffic and affecting the emergency services. The protestors are now committing the offence of "participating in an unauthorized assembly". Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and respect other people's rights while expressing their views.  Police appeal to everyone at scene to leave immediately.  Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Sunday, August 11, 2019
Issued at HKT 17:10
