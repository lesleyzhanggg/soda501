Police conduct dispersal operation in Wong Tai Sin and Yuen Long and report room services of Wong Tai Sin Police Station suspended
Police conduct dispersal operation in Wong Tai Sin and Yuen Long and report room services of Wong Tai Sin Police Station suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors have been blocking Lung Cheung Road, Wong Tai Sin and Castle Peak Road, Yuen Long, paralysing traffic in the vicinity.
Having given repeated warnings to the protestors in vain, Police officers are now deploying appropriate force to conduct a dispersal operation.
Police appeal to everyone on site to leave immediately.
The report room services of Wong Tai Sin Police Station are now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicle access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Saturday, October 5, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:20
