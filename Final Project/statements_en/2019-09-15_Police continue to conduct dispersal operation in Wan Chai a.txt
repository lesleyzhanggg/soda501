Police continue to conduct dispersal operation in Wan Chai and Causeway Bay
Police continue to conduct dispersal operation in Wan Chai and Causeway Bay
***************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors are still gathering at multiple locations in Wan Chai and Causeway Bay, causing a number of violent clashes. Police dispersal operations are still in progress. Multiple locations in the vicinity were blocked by barricades, causing serious obstruction to the traffic.
Police warn protestors in Wan Chai and Causeway Bay to leave immediately and remind members of the public to avoid travelling to the area.
Ends/Sunday, September 15, 2019
Issued at HKT 19:53
