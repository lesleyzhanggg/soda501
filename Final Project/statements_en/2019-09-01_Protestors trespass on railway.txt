Protestors trespass on railway
******************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 4pm today (September 1), a large group of protestors hurled numerous iron poles, bricks and rocks onto the track near the Airport Station of the Airport Express. Some even trespassed on the track, seriously obstructing train services.
Police will soon conduct a dispersal operation and warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Sunday, September 1, 2019
Issued at HKT 17:14
