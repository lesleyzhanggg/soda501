Tin Shui Wai Division Police Station Report Room service suspended
Tin Shui Wai Division Police Station Report Room service suspended
******************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large number of protestors are currently gathering outside Tin Shui Wai Division Police Station. For safety reasons, the report room services of Tin Shui Wai Division Police Station are now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicles access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, August 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 23:17
