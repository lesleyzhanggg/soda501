Kwun Tong Police Station Report Room service suspended
Kwun Tong Police Station Report Room service suspended
******************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some protestors gathered outside Kwun Tong Police Station, spraying paint onto the outer wall and hurling miscellaneous objects into the station, causing multiple damages.
For safety reasons, the report room services of Kwun Tong Police Station are now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicles access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, August 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 23:03
