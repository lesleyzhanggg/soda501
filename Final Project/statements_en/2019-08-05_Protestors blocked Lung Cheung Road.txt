Protestors blocked Lung Cheung Road
***********************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 3.15pm, protestors gathering in the vicinity of Lung Cheung Road near Temple Mall blocked the carriageway for both Eastbound and Westbound of Lung Cheung Road, seriously paralysing the traffic.
Police warn protestors to stop blocking the roads and other illegal acts immediately.
Police appeal to the members of the public to stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Monday, August 5, 2019
Issued at HKT 18:29
