Rioters block Tin Sau Road in Tin Shui Wai with miscellaneous objects
*********************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 6.30pm today (February 15), rioters occupied Tin Sau Road in Tin Shui Wai and built barricades with rubbish bins, wooden boards, foam boxes and debris to block multiple carriageways. Vehicles passing through the carriageways rolled over these miscellaneous objects. Such acts paralysed traffic in the area and posed a grave threat to road safety.
Police swiftly attended the scene to remove the trash. Police will not condone any unlawful act and warn rioters to stop breaching public peace.
Ends/Saturday, February 15, 2020
