Eighteen nomination forms for Legislative Council General Election received

The nomination period for the 2020 Legislative Council General Election starts today (July 18) and runs until July 31. A total of 18 nomination forms were received by the Returning Officers for the five geographical constituencies and the 29 functional constituencies on the first day of the nomination period.
