Protestors gather and chant slogans at a shopping mall in Kwun Tong
*******************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 6pm today (June 30), some protestors were gathering and chanting slogans at a shopping mall in Kwun Tong.  Police officers have repeatedly warned the crowd that they may commit relevant offences under the Public Order Ordinance.
Police appeal to the protestors to stop gathering and leave immediately. Police have zero tolerance towards illegal acts and will take enforcement action resolutely.
Ends/Tuesday, June 30, 2020
