Police conduct dispersal operation in Tsim Sha Tsui
Police conduct dispersal operation in Tsim Sha Tsui
***************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Radical protestors set fire and threw petrol bombs at Nathan Road in Tsim Sha Tsui, posing a serious threat to the safety of everyone, including police officers at scene. Police deploy appropriate force to disperse the protestors. The Police warn protestors to stop any illegal acts and leave immediately.
Police also remind the members of the public in the area to stay tuned to the latest situation, keep their windows shut and stay indoors, if necessary.
Ends/Saturday, August 31, 2019
Issued at HKT 22:03
