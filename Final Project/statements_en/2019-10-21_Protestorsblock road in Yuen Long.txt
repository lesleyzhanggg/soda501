Protestors block road in Yuen Long
Protestors
block road in Yuen Long
**********************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 7.30 pm today (October 21), some radical protestors set barricades and obstructed Castle Peak Road and Tai Tong Road in Yuen Long, seriously affecting the traffic. Police is now conducting dispersal operation.
Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave immediately. Police appeal to members of the public to be aware of the latest traffic situation.
Ends/Monday, October 21, 2019
Issued at HKT 20:29
