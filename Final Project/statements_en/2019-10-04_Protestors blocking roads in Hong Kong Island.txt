Protestors blocking roads in Hong Kong Island
*********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors have been occupying various major roads, including Pedder Street, Des Voeux Road Central, Man Yiu Street and Man Cheung Street, causing serious obstruction to the traffic. Some of them also set fire to a national flag. Police warn the protestors to leave and stop their illegal acts immediately.
Police appeal to drivers to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangements.
Ends/Friday, October 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 18:57
