Protestors block carriageways in Tin Shui Wai
*********************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 6:30pm today (September 14), some protestors were blocking part of the carriageways of Tin Wah Road and Tin Shui Road in Tin Shui Wai area and setting up barricades with trash, seriously paralysing the traffic.
Police warn protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave the scene immediately. Drivers are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Saturday, September 14, 2019
