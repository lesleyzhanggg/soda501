324 nomination forms for District Council Ordinary Election received

The nomination period for the 2019 District Council Ordinary Election starts today (October 4) and runs until October 17.  A total of 324 nominations were received by the Returning Officers of the 18 districts on the first day of the nomination period.

The Registration and Electoral Office reminds nominees that due to limited space in the Returning Officer's office, each nominee shall be accompanied by only one companion during submission of the nomination form. Please refer to the "Points to Note for Submission of Nomination Forms" on the election website
(
www.eac.hk/pdf/distco/2019dce/Points_to_Note_for_Submission_of_Nomination_Forms(Eng).pdf
).

).
