Mong Kok Police Station Report Room and MTR Hung Hom Station Police Reporting Centre services suspended
******************************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
The Report Room services of Mong Kok Police Station and MTR Hung Hom Station Police Reporting Centre are now temporarily suspended. Police appeal to members of the public not to obstruct the emergency vehicles access so as to avoid affecting the emergency services provided to the public. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Saturday, August 3, 2019
Issued at HKT 19:27
