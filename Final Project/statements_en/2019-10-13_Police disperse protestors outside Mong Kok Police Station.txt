Police disperse protestors outside Mong Kok Police Station
**********************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 10.30pm yesterday (October 12), a large group of protestors surrounded the Mong Kok Police Station, behaved in a disorderly manner, threw stones at the station and aimed laser beams at police officers. Such acts threatened the safety of members of the public and police officers.
After repeated warnings went futile, Police have deployed the minimum force necessary to disperse the protestors.
Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately. Members of the public are advised to avoid travelling to the area.
Ends/Sunday, October 13, 2019
Issued at HKT 0:00
