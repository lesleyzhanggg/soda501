"Book GOPC" mobile application function continues to roll out to more clinics
*****************************************************************************
The following is issued on behalf of the Hospital Authority:
The Hospital Authority (HA) is now rolling out the mobile application function "Book GOPC" in phases to clinics in more districts. Beginning today (September 11), members of the public can use the mobile application for appointment booking for all general out-patient clinics (GOPC) in North District, Sha Tin and Tai Po Districts. The HA plans to extend the coverage of the “Book GOPC” mobile application function to the remaining GOPCs in the New Territories (i.e. Tuen Mun and Yuen Long Districts) by end of September.
Clinic staff will assist patients in need to activate their "HA Go" accounts. The GOPC Telephone Appointment System continues to provide booking services as usual. For details of the booking means for GOPC services and other related information, including the tentative rollout schedule of the mobile application function "Book GOPC", please visit the GOPC website (
www.ha.org.hk/gopc
).
