Protestors block road in Yuen Long
**********************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 9pm today (October 26), some protestors gathered at Castle Peak Road - Yuen Long near Tai Tong Road. They first clamoured on the pavement but then some of them occupied the road, seriously obstructing traffic. Police arrived scene and ordered the protestors to return to the pavement and there was once a standoff between Police and the protestors.
Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts and leave immediately.
Ends/Saturday, October 26, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:52
