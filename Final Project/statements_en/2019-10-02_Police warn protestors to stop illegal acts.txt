Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts
Police warn protestors to stop illegal acts
*******************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large number of protestors are currently removing railings from roadsides and digging up bricks from pavements of Sha Tsui Road, Tsuen Wan. They are also occupying the roads nearby which paralysed traffic.
Police warn the protestors to stop all illegal acts immediately.
Drivers and members of the public are advised to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Wednesday, October 2, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:32
