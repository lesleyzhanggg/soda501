Police appeal for information on fatal traffic accident in Tseung Kwan O
************************************************************************
Police today (November 2) appealed to the public for information on a fatal traffic accident in Tseung Kwan O on October 26 in which a 35-year-old man died.
At about 11pm that day, a 35-year-old man riding a bicycle and the 35-year-old man riding an electric scooter were travelling in sequence along the cycling track of Tseung Kwan O Waterfront Park towards Tiu Keng Leng. When approaching the cycling track between Pung Loi Avenue and Lohas Park Road, the electric scooter and the bicycle reportedly collided.
Sustaining head injuries, the man was rushed to Queen Elizabeth Hospital in unconscious state and was certified dead at 2.50pm on October 30.
Investigation by the Special Investigation Team of Traffic, Kowloon East is underway.
Anyone who witnessed the accident or has any information to offer is urged to contact the investigating officers on 2305 7606 or 2305 7500.
Ends/Saturday, November 2, 2019
