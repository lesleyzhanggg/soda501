Protestors block carriageways in Central
****************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
A large group of protestors participating in a public event in Chater Garden are blocking the eastbound and westbound carriageways in Chater Road, Des Voeux Road Central, Connaught Road Central and westbound Harcourt Road flyover, seriously paralysing the traffic.
Police appeal to the protestors to respect the rights of other members of the public while expressing their views.
Members of the public are reminded to stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Monday, October 14, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:19
