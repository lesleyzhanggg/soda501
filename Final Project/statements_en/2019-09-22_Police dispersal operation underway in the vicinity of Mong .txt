Police dispersal operation underway in the vicinity of Mong Kok Police Station
Police dispersal operation underway in the vicinity of Mong Kok Police Station
******************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Radical protestors today (September 22) have been gathering on the roads in the vicinity of Mong Kok Police Station, throwing bricks at the police station and aiming laser beams at police officers at scene.
Police are using appropriate force to conduct a dispersal operation, and warn all protestors to stop all illegal acts and leave immediately.
Police remind the residents in the vicinity to stay tuned to the latest situation, stay indoors and keep their windows shut, if necessary.
Ends/Sunday, September 22, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:45
