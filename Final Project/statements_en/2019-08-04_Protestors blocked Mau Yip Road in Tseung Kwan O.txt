Protestors blocked Mau Yip Road in Tseung Kwan O
************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some protestors have removed railings from the kerbside and set barricades on Mau Yip Road outside the Metro City at MTR Po Lam Station, blocking roads, and seriously affecting the traffic in the area and emergency services provided to the public.
Police warn the protestors concerned to stop the obstruction and other illegal acts. Protestors are advised to respect others’ rights while expressing their views.
Police appeal to the members of the public to avoid travelling to the area and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Sunday, August 4, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:52
