Seize the opportunity and register online for CuMask+™
*********************************************************
The online registration period for the CuMask+™ will end on June 6. The Innovation and Technology Bureau (ITB) appealed to residents to seize the opportunity and register online (qmask.gov.hk).
As at 9am today (May 31), the CuMask+™ online registration system has received over 1.37 million registrations, covering more than 3.75 million registrants in total. Over 3.23 million masks have been delivered successfully by the Hongkong Post.
A spokesman for the ITB said today that the online registration for the CuMask+™ is quick and convenient. On average, citizens will receive masks delivered at door one week after registration. The spokesman added that both residential and commercial addresses are accepted. Citizens may consider providing a commercial address to facilitate collection of mail and seize the remaining week of registration period to register for themselves, family members and friends.
Citizens who do not register online may go to designated collection points in person and present their Hong Kong Identity Card to receive the CuMask+™ in due course. Details and the exact date will be announced later.
