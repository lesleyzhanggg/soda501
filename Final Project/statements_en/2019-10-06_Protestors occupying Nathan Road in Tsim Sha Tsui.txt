Protestors occupying Nathan Road in Tsim Sha Tsui
*************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 2pm today (October 6), a large number of protestors started occupying the northbound carriageway of Nathan Road in Tsim Sha Tsui. They are currently heading to Mong Kok direction, causing serious obstruction to the traffic. They are participating in an unauthorized assembly, which is an illegal act.
Police appeal to the protestors to leave immediately. Due to the current situation, drivers are advised to stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
The report room services of Tsim Sha Tsui Police Station are now temporarily suspended. In case of emergency, please call 999.
Ends/Sunday, October 6, 2019
Issued at HKT 15:05
