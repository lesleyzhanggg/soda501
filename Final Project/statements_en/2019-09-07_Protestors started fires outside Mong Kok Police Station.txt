Protestors started fires outside Mong Kok Police Station
********************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some protestors have started fires outside Mong Kok Police Station, posing a serious threat to the safety of everyone on site.
Having given repeated warnings to the protestors but in vain, the Police officers may deploy appropriate force to disperse the protestors.
The Police warn the protestors to stop their illegal acts immediately. The Police appeal to everyone on site to leave immediately.
Ends/Saturday, September 7, 2019
Issued at HKT 21:07
