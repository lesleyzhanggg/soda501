Protestors participating in unauthorized assembly in Tsim Sha Tsui
Protestors
participating in unauthorized assembly in Tsim Sha Tsui
******************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
At around 1:30pm today (October 20), some protestors gathering at Tsim Sha Tsui are blocking the carriageways on Nathan Road near Salisbury Road, and are committing the offence of “Participating in an unauthorized assembly”.
Police appeal to members of the public to leave immediately. Due to the obstruction of traffic, drivers are advised to stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Sunday, October 20, 2019
Issued at HKT 13:52
