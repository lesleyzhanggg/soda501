Protestors block roads with traffic cones in the vicinity of Sai Yeung Choi Street South in Mong Kok
******************************************************************************************
At about 8pm today (August 31), protestors have gathered and chanted slogans in Mong Kok. Some have blocked carriageways with traffic cones on Sai Yeung Choi Street South. Police at scene have repeatedly given warnings and taken resolute dispersal and arrest operations.
Police do not condone any illegal violent acts, and warn participants to leave immediately. Police would take resolute enforcement actions according to the situation at scene and bring offenders to justice.
Ends/Monday, August 31, 2020
