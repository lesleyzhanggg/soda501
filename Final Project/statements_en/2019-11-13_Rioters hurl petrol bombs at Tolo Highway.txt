Rioters hurl petrol bombs at Tolo Highway
Rioters hurl petrol bombs at Tolo Highway
*****************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Since last night (November 12), rioters have been occupying No.2 Bridge of the Chinese University of Hong Kong. They have hurled objects, bricks and petrol bombs at Tolo Highway underneath, seriously paralysing traffic and threatening the safety of the public. Rioters even threatened to hurl petrol bombs at all vehicles passing by.
Police warn the rioters to stop all illegal acts. As Tolo Highway is congested, drivers should avoid travelling to Tolo Highway and stay tuned to the latest traffic arrangement.
Ends/Wednesday, November 13, 2019
Issued at HKT 23:09
