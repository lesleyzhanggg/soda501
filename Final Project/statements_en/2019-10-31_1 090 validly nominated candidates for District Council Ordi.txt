1 090 validly nominated candidates for District Council Ordinary Election
*************************************************************************
The names of the 1 090 validly nominated candidates for the 2019 District Council Ordinary Election were gazetted today (October 31). The numbers for each candidate, which were determined by drawing lots, were also gazetted.
The 1 090 validly nominated candidates will compete for the 452 seats in the 18 districts.
"Polling will take place on November 24 (Sunday). Over 600 ordinary polling stations and 23 dedicated polling stations will be open on the polling day for over 4.13 million electors listed on the 2019 Final Register to cast their votes," a spokesman for the Registration and Electoral Office said.
The polling hours for the ordinary polling stations and the three dedicated polling stations set up at police stations will be from 7.30am to 10.30pm. For the 20 dedicated polling stations set up in penal institutions, for security reasons, the polling hours will be from 9am to 4pm.

In addition, the Electoral Affairs Commission (Electoral Procedure) (District Councils) (Amendment) Regulation 2019 has come into operation on October 18. The amendment regulation replaces the requirement of disclosing the "principal residential address" with "address" in the gazette notices in District Council Elections. Candidates may choose to disclose an address that they prefer, be it their office or business address, correspondence address, residential address or post office box number.
The gazette notices and information of the candidates are available on the election website (
