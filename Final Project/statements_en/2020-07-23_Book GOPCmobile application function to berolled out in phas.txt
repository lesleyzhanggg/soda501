"Book GOPC" mobile application function to be rolled out in phases
"Book GOPC"
mobile application function to be
rolled out in phases
******************************************************************
The following is issued on behalf of the Hospital Authority:
The Hospital Authority (HA) will roll out the "Book GOPC" mobile application function in phases from tomorrow (July 24). The mobile application function will then cover general out-patient clinics (GOPCs) on Hong Kong Island and in parts of Islands District (namely Peng Chau, Lamma Island, and Cheung Chau) (see table below) for members of the public to book appointments in GOPCs.
The HA launched a pilot programme for the "Book GOPC" mobile application function on June 19, providing an additional means for members of the public to book appointments in GOPCs, on top of the existing GOPC Telephone Appointment System. With the smooth implementation of the pilot programme and the function being well received by patients, the HA will roll out the "Book GOPC" mobile application function in phases.
The spokesperson said, "The public can download the 'HA Go' mobile application, complete the registration and activate their accounts at the designated counters during their next appointments, after which they can use the "Book GOPC" function in the mobile app." The spokesperson added that the function will gradually be implemented in Kowloon and the remaining parts of the New Territories with the aim of covering all GOPCs by the end of September.
Clinic staff will assist patients in need to activate their accounts. The GOPC Telephone Appointment System continues to provide its booking service as usual. For details of the booking means for GOPC services and other related information, please visit the GOPC website (
www.ha.org.hk/gopc
).
