District Council Ordinary Election provisional voter turnout
************************************************************
A total of about 2,940,000 registered electors have cast their votes in the 2019 District Council Ordinary Election yesterday (November 24), representing a provisional cumulative turnout rate of about 71.2%.
The voter turnout figures are for temporary reference only.
Ends/Monday, November 25, 2019
