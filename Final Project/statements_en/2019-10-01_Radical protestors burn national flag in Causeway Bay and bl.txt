Radical protestors burn national flag in Causeway Bay and block roads in Sheung Wan
Radical protestors burn national flag in Causeway Bay and block roads in Sheung Wan
***********************************************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some radical protestors burned a national flag at the tram station in the junction of Hennessy Road and Percival Street, in Causeway Bay.
Some protestors set barricades with trash at Connaught Road Central outside Sheung Wan Shun Tak Centre, causing obstruction to traffic.
Police warn everyone at scene to stop their illegal acts and leave immediately.  Police strongly condemn all illegal acts by the protestors.
Ends/Tuesday, October 1, 2019
Issued at HKT 16:12
