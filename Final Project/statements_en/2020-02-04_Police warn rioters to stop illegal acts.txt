Police warn rioters to stop illegal acts
****************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
As the night fell today (February 4), masked rioters wreaked havoc at multiple locations, which included blocking Tong Tak Street in Tseung Kwan O, vandalising a shop in Shatin Plaza and throwing bricks outside Tin Shui Shopping Centre in Tin Shui Wai, posing a serious threat to public safety and public order.
Police warned the rioters to stop all illegal acts and have taken resolute enforcement actions accordingly.
Ends/Tuesday, February 4, 2020
Issued at HKT 23:50
