32 more nomination forms for District Council Ordinary Election received
************************************************************************
A total of 32 nomination forms for the 2019 District Council Ordinary Election were received by the Returning Officers of the 18 districts today (October 11).
This brings the total number of nomination forms received to 828 since the start of the nomination period on October 4, which will continue until October 17.
A spokesman for the Registration and Electoral Office advised nominees to submit their nomination forms as early as possible to allow ample time to correct mistakes, if any, in the nomination forms before the deadline.
