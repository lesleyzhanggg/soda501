Masked rioters set fires in Yuen Long and Tseung Kwan O
*******************************************************
Attention duty announcers, radio and TV stations:
Please broadcast the following message as soon as possible and repeat it at suitable intervals:
Some masked rioters are currently gathering in Yuen Long and Tseung Kwan O, setting fire to miscellaneous objects on the road. Explosions were heard. In Mong Kok, some are still illegally occupying roads and paralysing traffic. Since around 9pm yesterday (October 7), Police officers have given repeated warnings to disperse the protestors but they still refused to leave. Police warn all protestors to leave immediately and stop all illegal acts. Officers will conduct arrest operations.
Ends/Tuesday, October 8, 2019
Issued at HKT 0:26
