Traffic enforcement operation against illegal parking in Sha Tin Police District
********************************************************************************
Sha Tin Police District launched a three-day traffic enforcement operation against illegal parking in Sha Tin, Tin Sum and Ma On Shan police divisions starting from today (March 17), to ensure smooth traffic flow and raise the safety awareness of road users.
Stringent enforcement actions will be taken during the operation. The Police will issue fixed penalty tickets to drivers who park their vehicles illegally, particularly those involve double parking, loading, unloading or waiting at restricted zones (e.g. near bus stops and crossing facilities), and obstructing emergency vehicular accesses.
The Police will issue multiple tickets and take summons actions against drivers who have contravened illegal parking regulations, obstructed traffic flow or caused danger to other road users. Illegally parked vehicles will be towed away if necessary.
